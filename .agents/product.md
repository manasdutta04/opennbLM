# Product

## Vision

Help people learn difficult subjects through patient, adaptive conversation that explains ideas clearly, checks understanding, and builds durable learning habits without pretending to replace teachers or authoritative sources.

## Non-goals

- A general-purpose autonomous agent or search engine.
- A grading authority, medical/legal/financial advisor, or source of professional certification.
- Silent profiling, indefinite transcript retention, or memory without user control.
- Lock-in to one LLM, speech provider, UI framework, or deployment vendor.
- Building product behavior during this reconnaissance/foundation phase.

## Product principles

Learner agency, clarity over theatrics, calibrated uncertainty, source-aware explanations, progressive difficulty, accessibility, privacy by default, and graceful failure when providers are unavailable.

## Current implementation status

No product flows, screens, APIs, or business functionality exist yet.
